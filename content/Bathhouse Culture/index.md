## Yes, Bathhouse Culture exists

Those of us who have been going to gay bathhouses for a while know how things work, what people expect, how to behave, and so on. We also understand some terms (words and phrases) and how they are used in the [[Glossary#Baths|baths]]. That all adds up to something very real: **Bathhouse Culture.**

Bathhouse Culture includes:
- Certain expectations, including [[Bathhouse etiquette|etiquette]] and [[Bathhouse rules|rules]]
- Common understandings, such as shared [[Glossary|language]]
- A rich and developing [[The History of Bathhouses|history]]
- Plenty of movies (and yes, most of them are porn)
- [[CumUnion and the Baths|Events]]
- Rich interrelationships with other [[Glossary#Subculture|queer subcultures]]
- Our own jokes and [[Disco-era rules of the baths|humor]]
- Even some of our own [[Glossary#Slurp ramp|architectural features]]

**Bathhouse Queen** came into existence to observe and document that culture -- particularly in a way that is clear and accessible for [[Field guide to your first visit|first-time visitors]] to the bathhouse.
