Not everything about bathhouses fits into a neat “how-to.”

Sometimes it’s worth stepping back and asking:

- What’s changing?
- Why does it feel different now than it did before?
- And what does that mean for those of us who enjoy these spaces?

This section of the site is where I think out loud.

Some of these are observations.  
Some are opinions.  
Most are a bit of both.
With plenty of my usual wit and tongue-in-cheek humor.
